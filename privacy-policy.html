Privacy Policy for PlyWhy?

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

PlyWhy? ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices and network handling for the PlyWhy? mobile application on Android.

2. Information Collection and Network Transmission

A. Public Chess Platform Synchronization

PlyWhy? allows you to import public chess game histories from Lichess or Chess.com:

  • Lichess API Requests: When you enter a public Lichess username in "My Games", the app sends HTTPS requests directly to https://lichess.org/api/games/user/{username}.
  • Chess.com API Requests: When you enter a public Chess.com username in "My Games", the app sends HTTPS requests directly to https://api.chess.com/pub/player/{username}/games.
  • Google Play Classification: Transmitting a public username off-device over HTTPS constitutes data collection under the User Info → User IDs / Personal Identifiers category.

B. Credentials and Sensitive Data

  • No Passwords or Tokens: PlyWhy? does NOT request, collect, transmit, or store Lichess or Chess.com account passwords, private OAuth tokens, or login credentials.
  • No Account Registration: You can use the app without registering an account.
  • No Location or Financial Data: We do not collect GPS location, financial information, or device contact lists.

C. Third-Party Trackers

PlyWhy? contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or commercial behavioral analytics SDKs.

3. Local On-Device Processing and Storage

  • Local Engine Analysis: All chess move safety evaluations, blunder detection, and training stats are processed 100% locally on your device via an embedded C++ Stockfish 18 engine (libstockfish.so). Position data is never uploaded to external analysis servers.
  • Local Data Persistence: Public usernames and imported game records are saved locally in on-device storage (FlutterSharedPreferences).

4. User Data Deletion

You can delete all locally cached usernames, imported games, and training statistics at any time through your Android device's System Settings → Apps → PlyWhy? → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
